1. Introduction

Ashtar Infinity ("we," "us," or "our") owns and operates Azadari Pak, an Android mobile application built with Flutter. Azadari Pak is a religious and community app that allows users to sign up, log in, browse majlis and program listings, post programs with poster images, view live program links, receive notifications, and manage their own posts.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how it may be shared, and how users can request deletion of their account and data.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use Azadari Pak:

  • Name.
  • Phone number.
  • Email address, if you provide one.
  • Gender.
  • Country, state, and city.
  • Profile image, if you upload one.
  • Program details posted by you, including title, date, time, address, phone number, notes, audience, live links, images, and posters.
  • Organizer image, if uploaded.
  • Location coordinates, only when you select or share a program location.
  • Device notification token used for push notifications.
  • Technical data needed for login, account security, app operation, moderation, and support.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create accounts and allow users to log in.
  • Verify accounts and support account recovery.
  • Show relevant male and female program listings.
  • Allow users to post, update, manage, and delete their own programs.
  • Send city-based notifications and live-program notifications.
  • Provide moderation, support, safety, fraud prevention, and security.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.

4. Public Content

Program posts submitted in Azadari Pak may be visible to other app users. Public program content may include the program title, date, time, address, phone number, notes, audience, images, posters, organizer image, selected location, and live program links.

Please do not include private or sensitive information in a public program post unless you want that information to be visible to other app users.

5. Location Data

Azadari Pak uses location features to help users select and preview program locations. We collect location coordinates only when you choose to select or share a location for a program.

Location data is used to display the program location and help users understand where the program is taking place. The App does not use location data to sell personal information.

6. Images and Uploaded Content

You may upload profile images, organizer images, program posters, and other program-related images. Uploaded content is stored so the App can show your profile or program listings.

Images connected to public program posts may be visible to other app users. You are responsible for making sure you have the right to upload and share any images or content you post.

7. Push Notifications

Azadari Pak may store a device notification token to send push notifications. Notifications may include city-based program alerts, live-program alerts, account messages, and other important app updates.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

8. Third-Party Services

Azadari Pak uses third-party service providers to operate important app features:

  • Supabase for user profiles, program data, storage, and backend functions.
  • Firebase for phone OTP authentication and push notifications.
  • Mapbox and geolocation services for selecting and previewing program locations.

These providers may process data on our behalf according to their own privacy policies, security practices, and service terms.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

We may share or make information available in the following cases:

  • Public program posts may be visible to other app users.
  • Data may be processed by Supabase, Firebase, Mapbox, and similar service providers that help us operate the App.
  • Information may be used or disclosed when needed for account verification, account recovery, moderation, support, security, legal compliance, or protection of users and the App.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep account and program information for as long as needed to provide the App, maintain security, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and operate support or moderation processes.

Users can request or delete their account from inside the App settings. Deleted accounts are soft-deleted first and scheduled for permanent deletion after 30 days. Programs from deleted users are hidden from public listings.

Some information may be retained for a limited time if needed for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, backups, or dispute resolution.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet or mobile app service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Children's Privacy

Azadari Pak is intended for general community use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without guardian permission. If we learn that a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the effective date on this page. Continued use of Azadari Pak after an updated policy is posted means you accept the updated policy.
