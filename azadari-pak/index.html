About Azadari Pak

Azadari Pak helps communities organize and discover Majalis, Juloos, and other Azadari programs by city and country.

The app is built for respectful community use. It supports local program discovery, public program posts, live links, and notifications so users can follow relevant Azadari activities in their area.
