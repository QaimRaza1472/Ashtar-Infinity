Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Ashtar Infinity ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Azadari Pak mobile application (the "App"), which allows users to browse, post, and manage religious and community program listings. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Azadari Pak, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: name, gender, country, state, city, phone number, optional email address, optional profile image, and a PIN for account access.
  • Program content you post: poster images, organizer name and/or image, program date and time, location details, address, map coordinates, contact details, notes, and live stream links.
  • Location data, only when you choose to add a map pin or current location to a program post.
  • Device information used to deliver push notifications (device tokens).
  • Technical and usage information needed for the App to function, such as login sessions and app activity logs.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Verify your identity through phone number and OTP
  • Allow you to post and browse program listings
  • Display program locations on a map
  • Send you notifications about programs and account activity
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of the App
  • Respond to support requests

4. Account and Login Data

To create an account, you provide your name, gender, country, state, city, phone number, an optional email address, an optional profile image, and a PIN. This information is used solely to manage your account and personalize your experience within the App. PINs are not stored in plain text; they are protected using secure hashing methods before being saved.

5. Phone Number and OTP Verification

Your phone number is used to log in to your account, verify your identity, send one-time passwords (OTP) for verification, assist with PIN recovery, and notify you of important account actions. We do not use your phone number for marketing or share it with advertisers.

6. Profile Images, Organizer Images, and Program Posters

You may optionally upload a profile image and, when posting a program, an organizer image and a poster image. These images are stored securely and may be visible to other users of the App as part of your profile or program listing, depending on where you choose to display them.

7. Location Data

Azadari Pak only accesses location data when you actively choose to add a map pin or your current location to a program post. We do not track or collect your location in the background, and location access is entirely optional and user-initiated.

8. Program Posts and Public Content

When you create a program listing, the information you provide (such as poster image, organizer name/image, date, time, country/state/city, address, map coordinates, contact details, notes, and any live stream links) may be visible to other users browsing the App. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in public program posts that you do not want others to see.

9. Notifications and Device Tokens

To send you push notifications about programs and account updates, we store a device token associated with your device. This token does not identify you personally and is used only for delivering notifications through our notification service.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to any third party. We do not use your personal data for advertising purposes. We only share information with trusted service providers who help us operate the App (such as cloud storage, authentication, and mapping providers), as described in the section below.

11. Third-Party Services

Azadari Pak relies on the following third-party services to operate:

  • Firebase — used for phone number OTP verification and push notifications.
  • Supabase — used for account management, profile data, program data, image storage, and other backend services.
  • Mapbox — used to display map previews and program locations.
  • YouTube — live stream links added by users may display YouTube content within the App.

These providers may process your data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy and security practices.

12. Data Retention

We retain your account and program data for as long as your account remains active and as necessary to provide our services. If you delete your account, your data is handled as described in the "Account Deletion" section below.

13. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time directly from within the App by going to Settings > Delete Account. When you request deletion, your account is first deactivated. It may then be permanently deleted from our systems after a retention period, which allows us to handle any pending issues (such as fraud prevention or legal obligations) before final removal.

14. Children's Privacy

Azadari Pak is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete such information.

15. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. This includes secure storage practices and hashing of sensitive credentials such as PINs. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal or regulatory reasons. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
