Customer policies

Policies, terms, and service information.

These policies apply to software development, website development, mobile app development, data science, AI, dashboard, backend, and support services provided by Ashtar Infinity.

Privacy

Privacy Policy

We collect only the information needed to respond to inquiries, prepare proposals, deliver services, process payments, and provide support. Client data, project files, and contact details are kept confidential and are not sold to third parties.

Refunds

Return / Refund Policy

Because our services are digital and custom-built, refunds are reviewed case by case. Approved refunds apply only to undelivered work or cancelled milestones before work begins. Completed design, development, consultation, and deployment work is non-returnable.

Delivery

Shipping / Service Policy

No physical shipping is involved. Project deliverables are provided digitally through email, cloud links, repositories, hosting environments, app builds, or agreed deployment channels. Timelines are confirmed in each proposal or invoice.

Terms

Terms & Conditions

Clients are responsible for providing accurate requirements, content, approvals, and account access. Work begins after agreed confirmation or payment. Final ownership of custom deliverables transfers after full payment, except third-party tools, licenses, and open-source components.
