Delete Your Azadari Data

Azadari allows users to delete individual programs they have posted without deleting their account.

How to Delete a Posted Program

  1. Open the Azadari app.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Open My Programs from the app drawer.
  4. Select the program you posted.
  5. Tap Delete Program.
  6. Confirm deletion.

Data Deleted When a Program Is Deleted

  • Program title and details
  • Program date and time
  • Program location and address
  • Program contact information
  • Poster/ishtiar images
  • Organizer image, if added
  • Live links attached to that program, if any
  • Public listing of that program

Data Not Deleted

  • Your Azadari account remains active.
  • Your profile information remains active.
  • Other programs you posted are not deleted.
  • Notification preferences are not deleted.

Retention Note

Some data may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, abuse prevention, backups, or system logs.

Full Account Deletion

If users want to delete their full Azadari account and associated account data, they can do this in the app:

Azadari app -> Settings -> Delete Account

Full account deletion details are available at https://ashtarinfinity.com/azadari-pak/account-deletion.
